Updated Criteria for Changes to International Rebate

Published: 19 December 2025

On 7 November 2025, the Minister for Economic Growth announced updates to the International Rebate, effective for productions starting principal photography or PDV Activity on or after 1 January 2026:

Lower Minimum Spend for Feature Films

The minimum spend for theatrically released feature films will reduce from NZ$15 million to NZ$4 million, aligning with the existing threshold for TV and streaming productions.

Removal of Above-The-Line Cap

The cap on above-the-line costs will be removed.

Updated 5% Uplift for Live Action Productions

The minimum spend required for Live Action productions applying for the 5% Uplift will decrease from $30 million to $20 million. This uplift will now be known as the Production Rebate 5% Uplift.

New 5% Uplift for PDV Productions

A new incentive, the PDV Rebate 5% Uplift, will be introduced for PDV Productions.

The updated Criteria, FAQs, application forms and other information are available at: https://www.nzfilm.co.nz/incentives/rebate-international-nzspr
